Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice describes current site and app behavior. The publisher should review it against each release and service configuration before publishing updates.

This notice covers the PesaPlanner website and the PesaPlanner Android money-management app. Privacy and support requests: mweshimiwaenterprises@gmail.com.

Website

The website stores your language, theme and cookie-consent choices in your browser. Optional Google Analytics is not loaded unless you allow analytics. If allowed, Google Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers to measure visits and page interactions; you can reject or withdraw consent using Cookie settings.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google and may share technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. GitHub Pages hosting may process standard connection and security logs.

If you submit a rating or review, its content, rating and form metadata are sent to FormSubmit for forwarding to the publisher by email. An email address is optional and is used if you ask for a reply. FormSubmit states it retains submissions for up to 30 days; the forwarded email may remain in the publisher's mailbox until deleted. Do not include sensitive financial information.

App records on your device

PesaPlanner's core money-management features work offline. Transactions, budgets, savings goals, bills, debts, categories and profile details are stored in a local Room database. These records are not synced to a PesaPlanner account or cloud service. The database does not use app-level database encryption. Android's application sandbox and device security still apply, but are not a PesaPlanner encryption guarantee. An app lock does not encrypt the database.

SMS and notifications

If you enable SMS import and grant Android's SMS permission, the app reads matching M-Pesa messages from shortcode 60000, parses them on your device and saves recognized transactions locally. This import flow does not upload raw SMS message bodies. If you enable notification access, Android lets the app inspect posted notifications; the listener filters for the Safaricom M-Pesa app and parses transaction text locally. These are optional sensitive permissions and can be revoked in Android Settings.

Files and exports

Backups and CSV exports contain readable financial information. You choose whether to share or save them; copies may remain with you or recipients. Delete copies you no longer need. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the current manifest, but this does not remove files you exported manually.

Paid plans and payment information

When you use subscription checkout in the Android app, the app sends checkout details to the PesaPlanner service over HTTPS and opens Paystack mobile-money checkout. The service stores the checkout email and phone, selected tier, amount, duration, currency, billing type, payment reference and status, payment/verification timestamps, a hashed one-time checkout access token, an issued license token and, where device restore is used, a hashed device identifier. Paystack processes payment details under its own terms. The service does not receive your local transaction ledger, budgets, SMS or imported statements. Your payment PIN is entered only in the payment provider's prompt and should never be sent to PesaPlanner support. The service has no self-service deletion feature; contact us to request access, correction or deletion. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting or legal reasons.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Providers process information needed to host the site, deliver analytics when you opt in, handle review submissions, or operate payments. Those providers may process information outside Kenya under their own terms. Local records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. This does not delete exports or server-side payment records. We retain payment-service records according to operational and applicable record-keeping needs; contact us for a request.

Your choices and requests

You can reject or withdraw website analytics consent. In the app, use available deletion controls or clear app storage/uninstall to remove local records; exported copies and payment-service records are separate. For access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy requests, email mweshimiwaenterprises@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification. Never send your M-Pesa PIN, password or full payment credential.

Security, children and updates

The app uses HTTPS for configured subscription requests, disables Android automatic backup and disallows cleartext network traffic in the current manifest. The local database is not app-level encrypted; exported files are readable. Do not use the app as your only record or store exports where others can access them. The publisher should confirm intended age groups and update this notice when data practices change.
