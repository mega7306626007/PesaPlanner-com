The problem · 01

Campus life is expensive.
Being broke is a full-time course.

University life in Kenya brings lectures, assignments, transport, food and bills. Keeping track of money becomes another full-time job.

M-Pesa messages = archaeology

Hundreds of messages. Deposits, payments, fees, airtime — somewhere between last week's shopping and a transaction you can't remember.

Balance ≠ spending money

KSh 5,000 in your account doesn't mean KSh 5,000 to spend. Rent, food, fare and bills are already spoken for.

Small expenses team up

One snack, one fare, one data bundle — affordable alone. Together across a month, your money does disappearing acts.

Friends' debts vanish

"Bro, nitakurudishia kesho." Tomorrow becomes next week. By semester end, your money graduated without you.

Unexpected bills attack

Rent deadlines, tokens, a broken charger — life introduces expenses when your account is least prepared.

Financial confusion steals peace

You should be thinking about your degree. Instead you're reconstructing last month's spending.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is my data safe?

Your records stay on your device. No mandatory cloud account. Analytics only load after your consent.

Does it work offline?

Yes — core money management works without internet. M-Pesa payment for subscriptions requires connectivity.

When is the APK available?

After a release is ready. The official download link will appear on this page. Avoid forwarded or re-uploaded files.

Can I use it for business?

Yes — business cash-flow, stock, customer balances and receivables are supported in the Max tier.

Personalization

Choose your setup. Categories follow.

Pick Student, Personal, Household or Business — the dashboard, navigation and quick-entry categories adjust to that starting point. Student setup asks about campus, housing and commuting; biashara gets sales and stock; household starts with home costs.

Distinct categories

Transaction categories are visually separated by setup: business sales never leak into personal spending, shared bills stay distinct from private purchases, and student costs (fees, fare, meals) get their own grouping. You can change your setup later without losing records.

Beyond personal

Money lives that aren't just yours.

PesaPlanner adapts to biashara and household money — same device, same privacy, different tools.

🏪

Business Desk

Record sales and costs, track stock by quantity, unit, buying cost and selling price. A sale updates stock and the ledger; restocking records the purchase. Keep business bills, customer and supplier contacts close by.

🏡

Household

Plan shared bills, contributions and family goals without pooling accounts. Keep personal transactions private; choose specific things to share. No shared logins or cloud sync required.

📦

Stock & Belongings

Track recorded food supplies and personal items. Check what you already have before buying more unga.

Legal & privacy

Your data, your control.

PesaPlanner is a personal finance tool, not a bank or lender. Records stay on your device. No mandatory cloud account. Analytics load only after your consent.
