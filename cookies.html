Cookies and browser storage

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The website stores your language choice in localStorage so your selected language is remembered. This is essential site functionality, not an advertising cookie.

Optional analytics

If you opt in, Google Analytics (measurement ID G-05FEPBJWY5) loads and may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browsers and measure site use. Google may process technical identifiers and visit information as a service provider. Analytics is off unless you choose “Allow analytics”. You may choose “Reject optional” without losing any site feature. Your choice is saved in localStorage as accepted or rejected; the site does not create an analytics cookie before consent.
