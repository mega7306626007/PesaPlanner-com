Delete your data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

PesaFlow does not currently require account creation or sync local financial records to a server. To remove records stored by the app, use available in-app deletion controls, or clear PesaFlow’s storage/uninstall it through Android settings. This also removes local preferences and records. It does not delete copies you exported, shared, or saved elsewhere.

If paid subscriptions are enabled, payment and entitlement records may be stored by the subscription service. Send a deletion request to [MONITORED PRIVACY / SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email or include enough information for us to verify the request. Never send an M-Pesa PIN, account password, or full recovery key. Certain transaction records may be retained when required by law; we will explain any exception.

Website language and optional-analytics choices can be cleared in browser site settings or via Cookie settings on our cookies page.
