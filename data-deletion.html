Delete your data

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Deleting local app records, exported files and payment-service records are separate actions.

Remove app data from your phone

Use available in-app deletion controls, or clear PesaPlanner's storage/uninstall it through Android settings. This removes app records and preferences stored locally. It does not delete CSVs or backups you exported, shared or saved elsewhere. Delete those files separately from each location or ask each recipient to remove their copy.

Request payment-service data deletion

If you used a paid plan, email mweshimiwaenterprises@gmail.com with a subject such as “PesaPlanner data request.” Tell us which email address or checkout phone you used, but never send your M-Pesa PIN, password, full checkout token or payment credential. We may ask for reasonable verification. Some payment or accounting records may need to be retained where required; we will explain if that applies. The app does not currently offer a self-service deletion control for payment-service records.

Website preferences

Clear site data in your browser to remove stored language, theme and analytics-consent preferences. You can also reopen Cookie settings to change analytics consent.
